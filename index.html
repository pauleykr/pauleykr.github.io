Post Cruise Nudge email

View Email Here
This email was designed around a simple behavioral insight: memories fade fast, but motivation to record them follows a predictable emotional arc. Rather than sending a single generic "share your trip" prompt, I helped design a two-part touchpoint strategy tied to where a guest was in their emotional journey — a mid-cruise email, sent while they still had time and energy to reflect in the moment, and a post-cruise nudge, timed a few days after return once the logistics of coming home had settled but nostalgia was still fresh. The goal was to catch guests in the window where sentiment was highest and friction was lowest, rather than competing with the flood of "welcome home" admin emails guests get immediately after a trip. This reflects the kind of audience-state thinking that sits behind good lifecycle and re-engagement strategy: not just what to say, but when a message will actually land.

Feature eNewsletter for Record My Cruise

View Email Here
This email served a dual purpose in the customer lifecycle: reactivating dormant accounts and driving feature adoption. Rather than a simple "here's what's new" announcement, the strategic goal was reminding users of an account relationship they may have forgotten about, then giving them a concrete reason to re-enter the product, new functionality they hadn't yet experienced. The underlying bet was that feature announcements are more effective as a re-engagement lever than as a pure product-update broadcast: users who left an account dormant need a reason to come back, and "something new exists that you haven't tried" is a lower-friction hook than a generic win-back message. This ties feature communication directly to activation and retention strategy, not just product marketing.
